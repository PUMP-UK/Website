Before you enquire

Do you work on period properties?

Yes. Period-property improvements form part of the work offered by Power Up My Property. The gallery includes an interior archway renovation and stonework.

Can I discuss electrical and plastering work together?

Yes. Power Up My Property offers both electrical work and plastering. Describe the work you need together when making your enquiry.

What information helps with a renovation enquiry?

Send the town or postcode, photographs, the rooms or areas involved, and a list of the changes you have in mind. If you have drawings or an intended timescale, mention those too.

Which areas do you cover?

Power Up My Property is based in Gloucestershire. Send Steve your town or postcode so he can confirm coverage for your particular project.
