Before you enquire

Can I see examples of your plastering?

Yes. This page shows the archway at three stages. The homepage gallery also includes wall preparation, room plastering, corners and wall finishing.

Do you plaster archways as well as walls?

Yes. The project photographs show both interior wall plastering and plaster being finished around an archway.

What should I send with a plastering enquiry?

Include your town or postcode, photographs of the walls or archway, approximate dimensions and the finish you want. Mention any related renovation work and your preferred timescale.

Which areas do you cover?

Power Up My Property is based in Gloucestershire. Send Steve your town or postcode so he can confirm coverage for your particular project.
